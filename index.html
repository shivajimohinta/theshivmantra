About

From the streets to the boardroom

A practitioner first. Every idea I share has been tested on real dealers, real architects & real monthly targets.

I started as a sales trainee at Philips in North Bihar, selling radios & black and white TVs to dealers. Over 36 years the journey took me through LML, BPL, Samsung, Hyundai & Fedders Lloyd in consumer durables, then into building materials & interior products, across regions & cultures.

In 2021 I co founded Dr Decor, a wooden flooring & decorative panels business. In 2023 we added Cub Crests, a specialty panels company. Both run on one contrarian playbook we call the 4S: Specification, Selective Distribution, Segregation & Scarcity. Most of the industry chases every dealer in town. We chase the right ones.

Today I also advise family & promoter led businesses. Most hit the same wall: how to build management systems & grow without the promoter's day to day involvement. Which 2 or 3 levers will really run the business? Every big customer still wants to speak only to "Sir." I help them build systems & strategies that run without the founder at every desk.

A promoter's real success is a business that runs well even when his phone is switched off.

The DRH Philosophy

Datte Rehna Hai.

Stand firm. Stay the course.

  • Stand firm when it gets tough.
  • Stay steady when results are slow.
  • Stay true when shortcuts tempt you.

Because giving up just before the right time is what separates the also ran from the winner.

The Shiv Mantras

Seven lines to carry back to work

Drawn from 36 years on the ground, the SMILE model & 15 Plus One Power Habits. Each mantra opens one of my talks.

  1. 01

    Ask IIM before IIT.

    Is It Me? before Is It Them?

    Before blaming the team, the dealer or the market, look in the mirror first. Most fixes start with the leader.

    A branch misses target & the manager blames the dealers. IIM asks: did I visit them, set the right scheme, train the team?

    SMILE · Self ManagementTalk · Leading with SMILE
  2. 02

    Sales isn't rocket science. It's neuroscience, with a dash of chemistry.

    Customers decide with the primal brain & justify with logic. Trust is pure chemistry. Are you addressing the primal brain?

    How neuromarketing helps you find the customer's buy button.

    SMILE · Master Your GameTalk · Neuromarketing way of Selling
  3. 03

    Aspirins are bought. Vitamins are sold.

    A customer in pain buys on his own. One who doesn't feel the pain yet needs a salesman who can uncover it & show what he would be missing.

    Help the customer turn a need into a want.

    SMILE · Master Your GameTalk · Selling with a Story
  4. 04

    A NO is never a no till it's Not Over.

    A first no is usually a “not yet.” Winners treat rejection as information, not a stop sign.

    The order rarely comes on the first visit. Often it comes on the seventh, the one most people never make.

    SMILE · Self ManagementTalk · Negotiation Techniques
  5. 05

    Tolerate a bad one, lose a good one.

    Your best people watch how you handle your worst. Silence on poor performance is a message too.

    The monkey who throws back the cucumber when his neighbour gets a grape. Fairness is felt, not announced.

    SMILE · Inspire PeopleTalk · Corporate talks · Winning Habits
  6. 06

    Decide at 24°, but check it at 44°.

    The AC cabin vs the market outside

    Plans made in the cabin must survive the heat of the market. Walk the ground before you sign the circular.

    A scheme that looked perfect on the boardroom screen, but stumbled at the dealer's counter.

    SMILE · Master Your GameTalk · Build & Manage a Sales Pipeline
  7. 07

    Products can be copied. PACE can't.

    People · Agility · Culture · Experience

    When ten brands sell the same products at almost the same price, the winner is the one whose people behave like owners.

    Grow big, but keep a start up's speed of response.

    SMILE · Enduring LegacyTalk · Influencer Loyalty · CRM
